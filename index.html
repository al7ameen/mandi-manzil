Our Story

We don't just serve food – we serve tradition, hospitality, and an unforgettable culinary experience.

Mandi Manzil was born out of a deep admiration for the rich, aromatic, and soulful dish known as Mandi – a traditional Arabic rice and meat preparation that originated in Yemen and spread across the Middle East. We wanted to offer an experience that stayed true to its origins while also appealing to the local palate. With that vision, we set out on a mission to be Kerala's go-to destination for real, slow-cooked, wood-fired Mandi.

What started as a humble venture has now grown into a well-recognized name across Kerala, loved by thousands of loyal customers. Our consistency in taste, hygiene, and hospitality has been the secret ingredient to our success.

01

Authentic Preparation

Every dish is prepared using time-honored techniques, traditional spices, and slow-cooking methods that bring out the rich flavors of Arabian cuisine.

02

Premium Ingredients

We use only the finest cuts of meat, long-grain basmati rice, and a carefully curated blend of spices for that signature Mandi aroma and taste.

03

Ambience & Hospitality

At Mandi Manzil, we strive to create a warm, family-friendly atmosphere that reflects the true spirit of Arabian dining.
